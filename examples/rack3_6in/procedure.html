Stitching procedure: tomato rack 6in

Tick each step as you go. Works offline; print it or prop a phone next to the machine.

1. Before you start

    • Thread 1: olive (#739a5f), suggested Madeira Polyneon 1968 Fair Green.
    • Thread 2: royal blue (#3953a4), suggested Isacord Polyester 3332 Forget Me Not.
    • Thread 3: white (#fcfcfb), suggested Isacord Polyester 0015 White.
    • Thread 4: red (#e92024), suggested Isacord Polyester 1704 Candy Apple.
    • Thread 5: dark green (#264725), suggested Robison-Anton Polyester 5849 Blue Spruce.
    • Thread 6: black (#090b09), suggested Isacord Polyester 0020 Black.
    • Bobbin thread: 60 wt pre-wound, white (light fabric; the back of the embroidery shows the bobbin colour).
    • Needle: 75/11 embroidery needle (sharp point); 90/14 on heavy canvas or denim. Start every project with a fresh needle.
    • Stabilizer: One layer of medium-weight tear-away under the fabric (two layers on light cotton or linen). Heavy canvas or denim is stable enough on its own with one layer.
    • Temporary spray adhesive (505 or similar) if the garment cannot be hooped and must be floated.
    • Small curved embroidery scissors for jump threads, a seam ripper, and a lint brush.

3. Thread-by-thread run sheet

One block per colour stop, in sewing order. The machine stops and trims at the end of each colour.

    • Thread the needle with this colour before pressing start. Hold the thread tail for the first few stitches.
    • This colour stitches: 8 small shape(s) stitched as satin-style fills (2 to 4 mm across); 57 satin column(s), 1.1 to 2.6 mm wide, 809 mm in total; bean (triple) running-stitch lines, 336 mm in total, for details thinner than 1.5 mm; 2 small patch(es) filling spots the main objects left uncovered.
    • 6180 stitches, about 10 min.
    • Watch: Narrow satin columns: watch for thread breaks or shredding; slow the machine if the thread frays.
    • Watch: Small satin-style shapes: check the edges stay crisp and the shape is not pulling narrow.
    • Watch: Outline lines: check they line up with the fills already stitched (registration). If they drift, the fabric slipped in the hoop.
    • Watch: 64 jump(s) inside this colour: trim the floating threads by hand at the stop (centre 4 mm, centre right 6 mm, centre right 8 mm, centre right 12 mm ...).
    • At the stop: the machine trims the colour automatically; this colour also has 64 jump(s) inside it that the machine does NOT trim. Cut the floating threads on top close to the fabric before continuing.
    • Thread change to stop 2 (royal blue): cut the thread near the spool, pull the old thread out forward through the needle (never back up through the tension discs), put on the new spool, thread the path, use the automatic needle threader, and hold the tail for the first stitches.
    • This colour stitches: 3 satin column(s), 2.2 to 2.3 mm wide, 378 mm in total.
    • 2147 stitches, about 3.6 min.
    • Watch: 2 jump(s) inside this colour: trim the floating threads by hand at the stop (lower left 44 mm, upper right 108 mm).
    • At the stop: the machine trims the colour automatically; this colour also has 2 jump(s) inside it that the machine does NOT trim. Cut the floating threads on top close to the fabric before continuing.

5. Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely causeFixparams.yaml setting
Puckering around fillsNot enough stabilizer, fabric hooped loose, or stitching too denseAdd a stabilizer layer, re-hoop drum-tight, reduce densityfill.row_spacing_mm (raise to 0.45), fill.underlay.*
Gaps between coloursPull compensation too low for this fabricIncrease overlap and pull compensation, test againgeneral.overlap_mm (0.5), fill.pull_compensation_mm, satin.pull_compensation_mm
Outline does not line up with the fillFabric shifted in the hoop, or fills pulled inRe-hoop with cut-away, more underlay, more compensationfill.pull_compensation_mm, fill.underlay.edge_run_always
Bird-nesting under the fabricTop thread not seated in the tension discs or take-up lever; thread caught on the spoolRethread the top from the start, check the spool unwinds freely(not a digitizing problem)
Thread breaks or shreddingDull or wrong needle, burr on the plate, too dense or too short stitchesNew needle, slow the machine, open the densitysatin.spacing_mm, fill.row_spacing_mm, general.min_stitch_length_mm
Bobbin thread showing on topTop tension too tight or bobbin too looseLower top tension one step; check the bobbin casesatin.spacing_mm (if only on satins)
Satin text unreadableLetters too small for the threadScale the design up or simplify the textdetail.min_text_height_mm, satin.min_width_mm, --size-mm
Underlay shows at the edgesUnderlay inset too small for this fabricIncrease the underlay insetsfill.underlay.inset_mm, satin.underlay.contour_inset_mm
Design off-centre on the garmentCentre mark or hoop alignment offRe-mark, re-hoop, use trace before stitching(machine positioning)
Jump threads left on topSingle-needle machine does not trim inside a colourTrim by hand at each stopsequence.max_travel_mm, sequence.travel_under_cover
